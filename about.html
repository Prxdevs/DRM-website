Our Story — Since 1970

Five decades of getting rice right.

Founded in 1970, DRM – Shree Dwarkesh Rice Mill has been a trusted name in the rice industry for over five decades. With a deep-rooted commitment to excellence, we specialise in delivering premium-quality rice that meets the highest standards of purity, taste and nutrition.

Our journey reflects a legacy of trust, consistency and customer satisfaction.

At DRM, we believe in combining traditional values with modern technology to ensure that every grain we produce adds nourishment and flavour to meals across the country.

Legacy since 1970

Rooted in tradition. Built for tomorrow.

Since 1970, Shree Dwarkesh Rice Mill has been deeply rooted in India's rich agricultural tradition. Founded on the values of hard work, honesty and commitment, we have evolved alongside the rice industry while staying true to our origins.

With over five decades of experience, we understand the balance between preserving traditional practices and embracing modern technology.

As we expanded, we focused on quality, customer trust and long-standing partnerships with farmers. We believe in ethical sourcing, sustainable farming and responsible production practices.

Our legacy is more than just rice — it's a story of trust, quality and a passion for excellence. Every grain we produce reflects our journey of growth and dedication, and we will continue to serve with the same integrity for generations to come.
