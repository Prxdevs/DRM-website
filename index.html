01 — Finest & purest quality

At Shree Dwarkesh Rice Mill, every grain is carefully selected, cleaned and processed with state-of-the-art technology — so its nutrition, aroma and taste stay intact, from farm to plate.

Our commitment to purity starts right from the sourcing of premium paddy from trusted farmers. No chemical polishing or artificial enhancements are used — just naturally rich, delicious rice.

Hygienic packaging ensures our rice reaches your kitchen in perfect condition. Whether it's for daily meals or special occasions, our rice adds unmatched flavour and texture to every bite. Customer satisfaction and well-being are at the heart of everything we do.

02 — Legacy since 1970

A journey of trust & excellence.

Since 1970, Shree Dwarkesh Rice Mill has been deeply rooted in India's rich agricultural tradition. Founded on the values of hard work, honesty and commitment, we have evolved alongside the rice industry while staying true to our origins. With over five decades of experience, we understand the balance between preserving traditional practices and embracing modern technology.

As we expanded, we focused on quality, customer trust and long-standing partnerships with farmers. We believe in ethical sourcing, sustainable farming and responsible production. Our legacy is more than just rice — it's a story of trust, quality and a passion for excellence that will continue for generations to come.
